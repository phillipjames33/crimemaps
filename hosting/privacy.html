Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · App IDs: com.crimemaps.crime_maps (Android) & com.crimemaps.crimeMaps (iOS)

Crime Maps is designed to minimize data collection while delivering real-time public safety awareness, civic infrastructure transparency, and localized independent music.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Data (Optional): You may browse the map anonymously without creating an account. If you sign in with Email/Password via Firebase Authentication, we store your Firebase user ID (UID), email address, display name, and subscription entitlement status in Cloud Firestore (users/{uid}).
  • Location Data (On-Device First): With your permission, Crime Maps accesses device GPS coordinates to center the map, query nearby public incident feeds by coarse geohash bounding box, play Geo-Radio tracks for your current metro area, and trigger civic safety awareness alerts for publicly mapped traffic cameras.
  • Zero Continuous Server Location Tracking: Public traffic camera alerts are computed 100% locally on your device using an on-device spatial cache (Hive). Your continuous GPS trail is never streamed or stored on Crime Maps servers.
  • Creator Uploads & Community Submissions: If you voluntarily upload an independent audio track or submit a historical neighborhood context note, we store your uploaded file metadata, creator attribution, copyright warranty attestation timestamp, and target zip/city identifier.
  • Purchase & Subscription Metadata: In-app subscriptions (Crime Maps Pro) and creator track boosts are processed natively by Apple App Store and Google Play Billing and synced via RevenueCat. Crime Maps never receives or stores your payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

  • Display publicly sourced municipal crime feeds and historical neighborhood context.
  • Deliver opt-in push notifications for high-severity incidents in subscribed metro topics via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Process 100% free record removal/expungement requests and DMCA takedown notices.
  • Verify active Pro entitlements to remove advertisements and unlock extended awareness layers.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, FCM): Backend hosting, authentication, and public feed ingestion.
  • Google Maps Platform: Dark-mode map rendering and public Street View imagery.
  • RevenueCat: Cross-platform Apple App Store and Google Play subscription validation.
  • Google AdMob (Free Tier Only): Banner advertisements displayed to non-Pro users. Pro subscribers experience zero ads.

4. Data Retention & 45-Day Automatic Record Expiration

Public arrest and booking incident documents ingested into Crime Maps carry a strict 45-day automatic Time-To-Live (TTL) and are automatically purged by Firestore once expired. Individuals can also request immediate, permanent, 100% free removal at any time via the Free Record Removal Portal.

5. Account Deletion & Your Privacy Rights

Instant In-App Account Deletion: Signed-in users can permanently delete their account and associated user profile document at any time inside the Crime Maps app by opening the Account sheet (top-right profile icon on the Map screen) and tapping Delete Account Permanently.

You may also request account deletion, data export, or correction without installing the app by visiting our Account Deletion & Removal Portal or emailing phillipojames@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Crime Maps is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable by law) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be sent to phillipojames@gmail.com.
