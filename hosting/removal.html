Free Record Removal & Account Deletion

Crime Maps never charges a fee to remove an arrest or booking record, and every user can permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time.

1. 100% Free Arrest / Booking Record Removal

Every person referenced in a public booking entry is presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law. All booking records expire automatically after 45 days via server-side TTL, and you may request immediate suppression at zero cost:

  • Fastest (In-App Instant Suppression): Open any booking record card in Crime Maps and tap Request Free Record Removal. Submitting a valid dismissal, expungement, sealed-record, or mistaken-identity notice triggers our automated submitMugshotRemoval workflow to immediately hide the entry and log an auditable compliance receipt.
  • Web / Email Removal Request (No App Required): Email phillipojames@gmail.com with the subject "Free Record Removal Request - Crime Maps" and include the incident ID (or city, approximate date, and title) along with your reason (expungement, dismissal, sealing, or correction). We process all removal requests free of charge.

2. Permanent User Account Deletion

In full compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play Data Safety requirements, you can permanently delete your Crime Maps account and associated profile data in either of two ways:

  1. Directly Inside the Crime Maps App: Tap the Account icon in the top-right corner of the Map screen, then select Delete Account Permanently and confirm. This immediately deletes your Cloud Firestore user profile (users/{uid}) and permanently removes your Firebase Authentication account.
  2. Web / Email Account Deletion Request: If you have already uninstalled Crime Maps, email phillipojames@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account with subject "Account Deletion Request - Crime Maps". We will delete your Firebase Authentication record and Firestore profile data and confirm completion within 7 days.

What Is Deleted: Your Firebase Auth credentials, email address, display name, and Firestore users/{uid} profile record. Note that active Apple App Store or Google Play subscriptions must still be canceled in your device's store settings to stop recurring billing.
