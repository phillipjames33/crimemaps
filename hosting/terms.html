Terms of Service & DMCA Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

By using the Crime Maps mobile or web application, you agree to these Terms of Service, our Civic Transparency Rules, and our DMCA Safe Harbor Policy.

1. Public Safety Information & Presumption of Innocence

Crime Maps displays publicly available municipal open-data crime feeds and historical neighborhood context for informational and educational situational awareness. Crime Maps is not an official law enforcement agency, emergency dispatch service, or consumer reporting agency under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA).

  • Presumed Innocent: Any individual referenced in an arrest or booking entry is presumed innocent until proven guilty in a court of law.
  • No FCRA Use: You may not use Crime Maps data to make decisions about employment, housing, credit, insurance, or tenant screening.
  • Emergency Services: Never rely on Crime Maps during an emergency. Always dial 911 or your local emergency number.

2. Civic Infrastructure & Anti-Doxxing Policy

Camera awareness layers in Crime Maps exist strictly for driver safety and civic transparency regarding publicly mapped municipal traffic cameras and OpenStreetMap civic infrastructure.

  • Users must obey all posted traffic laws and operate mobile devices hands-free while driving.
  • Submitting, pinning, or mapping private residential security cameras, private doorbell cameras, or personally identifying residential addresses is strictly prohibited.

3. Historical Neighborhood Context

Neighborhood and territory overlays present neutral, historical cultural context sourced from moderated public records and archival references. Community context submissions enter a moderation review queue before publication and must never promote harassment, illegal activity, or violence.

4. Geo-Radio 100% Indie Creator Warranty

Creators uploading audio tracks to Crime Maps Geo-Radio affirmatively represent and warrant that:

  • They own or control 100% of both the master sound recording and the underlying musical composition/publishing rights.
  • The track contains no uncleared samples, beats, covers, or third-party copyrighted material.
  • They grant Crime Maps a non-exclusive, revocable license to stream the track to users within the creator's chosen geographic area.

5. Subscriptions & Creator Boosts

Crime Maps Pro auto-renewing subscriptions (Monthly or Annual) and consumable Creator Geo Boosts are billed through your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period in your device's store subscription settings.

6. DMCA Safe Harbor & Copyright Takedown Policy (17 U.S.C. § 512)

Crime Maps respects intellectual property rights and removes allegedly infringing audio or user submissions expeditiously upon receipt of a compliant DMCA notice. To submit a takedown notice, email phillipojames@gmail.com with subject line "DMCA Takedown Notice — Crime Maps" including:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. The track title, creator name, or specific location within Crime Maps sufficient to locate the material.
  3. Your contact information (name, address, phone number, and email).
  4. A statement of good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf, along with your physical or electronic signature.

Repeat infringers will have their uploader privileges permanently terminated.
